OUR STORY

GOOD CARS.
BETTER COMPANY.

Sundae Run began with a simple belief: car culture should be fun. No gatekeeping, no trying too hard. Just the machines we love and the people who make the miles matter.

What we stand for
THE VISION

A whole world, not just a shirt.

We're creating a brand that earns its place in car culture. We start with community and content. The shirts are a way to wear the feeling. The Garage game gives enthusiasts a little world to play in.

We'd rather build something people love over time than rush to sell something before the community exists.
