### Known visible errors

1. **Replacement rims do not fit:** The default rims are baked into the source reference. V2 overlays wheel-face replacements over the original photographed wheels, causing double spokes, the wrong perspective, mis-sized circles or misaligned hubs. The rear and front are **different perspectives** and cannot share one simplistic disc/crop without custom art.
2. **Paint preview looks pasted on/floating:** V2 paint effects were generated by rough color masks over pixels from a flattened screenshot, sometimes leaking to reflections, beyond the silhouette, or tinting inappropriate parts. The garage and car paint cannot be reliably separated with current assets.
3. **Selection checkmarks:** Original reference has baked-in turquoise check marks and borders, and V2 lays dynamic controls over them. Selecting another rim or black paint can leave ghost marks or inconsistent highlight positions. Need **real DOM selection indicators** and a clean static background with original UI selections removed, or wholly rebuilt live UI.
4. **General issue:** The default view uses a flattening trick: the entire car, platform, branding and menus are baked into a 1672×941 image with click hotspots and overlays. It can match the default image visually **but that is not proof of a functional, premium customisation system**.

See `qa/BROKEN_PAINT_AND_WHEELS_V2.png`, `qa/BROKEN_BLACK_PAINT.png`, `qa/BROKEN_MINT_PAINT.png`. Compare each to `reference/APPROVED_BLUE_STI_SCREEN.png`.

## 5. Recommended visual/technical strategy

**Do not rebuild the full screen yet. First solve the art impossibility.** The user wants exact scene fidelity and working wheel/paint preview, so choose an art pipeline that maintains the **same angle, lighting, wheelbase and backdrop** for all states.

**Preferred:** Reconstruct separately renderable scene layers: (1) empty branded workshop and platform; (2) fixed-angle STI body WITHOUT embedded wheels; (3) correctly projected separate front/rear wheel meshes or pre-rendered sprites; (4) paint-only body mask or independently rendered colour variants; (5) shadows/occlusion; (6) native live HTML/CSS HUD and bottom UI. Need high-quality source assets or art editing, not naïve mask-and-overlay scripts.

**Valid alternative:** If making fully independent layers to quality is impractical, create professionally matched, fixed-angle **complete STI variant renders** for a small curated set of wheel/paint combinations (blue/black/mint + two rim styles) on the same empty-workshop plate. This trades storage for consistency. Do not promise 6×12 viable combinations until the art exists.

**If neither is feasible:** Explain the missing production asset requirement (editable 3D source or high-resolution hand-produced layers) and present an asset procurement/creation plan. **Do not produce another car made of SVG, procedural low-poly, or off-angle stock-style overlay and call it complete.**

Use **Vite + HTML/CSS/JS** for page UI; use PixiJS 8 for multiple image layers and GSAP for smooth transitions only if it makes the result more reliable. Do not force unnecessary 3D. Keep the same 1672×941 internal stage coordinates and aspect-safe scaling.
